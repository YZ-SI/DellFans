1. IntelMSR.bin  (shipped next to the executables as a separate, unmodified file)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A PawnIO module (compiled AMX script) that lets the signed PawnIO driver read Intel model-specific
registers. DellFans loads it only after checking its SHA-256 and executes only the function
ioctl_read_msr, only for MSRs 0x19C, 0x1B1 and 0x1A2 (thermal readouts).

3. PawnIO driver (NOT bundled)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The kernel driver (PawnIO, (c) namazso) is NOT part of any DellFans archive and is not redistributed by
DellFans. On first run DellFans asks for consent; only after the user agrees (UAC prompt) it downloads the
pinned official PawnIO_setup.exe 2.2.0 from https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Setup/releases/tag/2.2.0,
verifies its SHA-256 and that the Authenticode signer is CN=namazso.eu, and only then runs it with
-install -silent. Uninstalling DellFans keeps PawnIO (other software may use it). PawnIO is licensed by its
author under its own terms; read them on the PawnIO project pages before installing it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LibreHardwareMonitor is not used or bundled (sensor reading is implemented in src\Sensors.cs); it is mentioned
